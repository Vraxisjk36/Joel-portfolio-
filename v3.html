I didn't grow up with a laptop. Getting my first one changed everything.

01 / Where I started

Born in the Democratic Republic of the Congo and raised in Kimberley, South Africa. I was fascinated by technology long before I had a computer. Towards the end of Grade 11, I finally got my first laptop.

02 / The first thing I built

In Grade 12 I built an NSC marks calculator that used actual assessment weightings to help matric students work out their results. I wanted to prove I could build software. What stuck with me was that something I made could make life easier.

03 / Learning by doing

I'm 20, currently in my second year of a BSc IT at Eduvos, specialising in Software Development. I study part-time and expect to graduate in 2027. Most of my practical knowledge is self-taught. I also collaborated on a student support platform at a university hackathon.

04 / How I work

I'm a creative problem solver and quick learner. I research, use AI to plan and explore alternatives, then build and refine. I enjoy pressure, complex challenges and working out problems from different angles.

05 / Beyond technology

I love watching and playing sports and serve as a small-group leader in my community. My favourite personal project is Vraxis Drop because I use it myself. When the going gets tough, the tough get going.
